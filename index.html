Unsaturated Hydrocarbon • C=C

Alkenes (Addition & Hydration)

View Full Mechanism & Structures →

Alkenes contain at least one carbon-to-carbon double bond (C=C). Because they are unsaturated, they undergo addition reactions where the double bond opens to join two new atoms across the carbons—converting two reactant molecules into a single saturated product molecule (100% atom economy).

Key Reactions

• Hydrogenation (+H₂, Ni catalyst → Alkane)
• Halogenation (+Br₂ → Dihaloalkane)
• Hydration (+H₂O, H₃PO₄ catalyst → Alcohol)

SQA Exam Example

Propene + H₂O produces a mixture of propan-2-ol (major product, 2° alcohol) and propan-1-ol (minor product, 1° alcohol).

Chemical Test

Rapidly decolourises orange/brown bromine solution (Br₂) to colourless at room temperature.

4-Stage Revision Plan

Check off each study milestone as you complete it. Your progress is automatically saved in your browser.

Stage 1: Foundations, Isomers & Addition Unit 2.1

Master systematic IUPAC naming up to C₈, structural isomerism, alcohol classification (1°, 2°, 3°), and addition/elimination reactions.

Stage 2: Oxidation, Carbonyls & Antioxidants Unit 2.2 & 2.5

Understand oxidation via O:H ratios, distinguish aldehydes from ketones, and memorise all four oxidising agent colour changes.

Stage 3: Esters, Fats, Oils, Soaps & Emulsifiers Unit 2.2, 2.3 & 2.6

Connect condensation and hydrolysis across flavour esters, triglyceride fats/oils, soap manufacture, and food emulsifiers.

Stage 4: Proteins, Terpenes & Exam Mastery Unit 2.4 & 2.7

Master peptide link formation/hydrolysis, protein denaturation, isoprene units in terpenes, and SQA 3-mark open-ended questions.

Esterification
Condensation 		Carboxylic Acid + Alcohol Conc. H₂SO₄ catalyst, warm water bath, wet paper towel condenser Ester + H₂O Sweet/fruity smell; oily immiscible layer on NaHCO₃(aq).
Ester Hydrolysis
Hydrolysis 		Ester + H₂O Dilute acid (H⁺) or alkali (NaOH), heat under reflux Alcohol + Carboxylic Acid (or carboxylate salt with NaOH) Alkaline hydrolysis is irreversible and gives higher yield.
